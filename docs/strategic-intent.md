# Strategic intent

Owner: Adamma Ihemeson. Version 1.0.

## What the AI is for

Workframe uses Claude for two jobs only:

1. Turning a student's written check-in note into a structured hours-saved estimate: the task, minutes before and after, how many times it ran, a confidence score and the exact phrases the numbers came from.
2. Drafting a short weekly summary of each cohort for its insider.

## What the AI must never do

- **Decide what counts as a result.** Any doubtful figure is held until a person approves it, and held figures count nowhere.
- **Message students with free-form text.** Every student email is a fixed template.
- **Identify a student in anything sent to an insider.** Each digest is checked for student names and emails before it is stored, and a match blocks it.
- **Have its numbers used in marketing or sales without a person approving them.**

Adamma Ihemeson owns this definition. Changing it is her decision.
