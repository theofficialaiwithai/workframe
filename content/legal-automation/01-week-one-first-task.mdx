# Your contract renewal watch list

Renewal dates hide in folders and spreadsheets until someone notices they passed. Today you will build a list that shows every contract renewing in the next 60 days, soonest first.

You will start from a sample sheet with a contract ID, a client, a renewal date and a value. The script keeps the contracts that renew soon and saves them as a short list you can review each Monday.

```python
import pandas as pd

contracts = pd.read_csv("sample.csv", parse_dates=["renewal_date"])
today = pd.Timestamp.today().normalize()
contracts["days_left"] = (contracts["renewal_date"] - today).dt.days

watch = contracts[(contracts["days_left"] >= 0) & (contracts["days_left"] <= 60)]
watch = watch.sort_values("days_left")
watch.to_csv("renewal_watch_list.csv", index=False)
print(watch)
```
