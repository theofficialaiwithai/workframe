# Your Monday lead follow-up list

Every Monday you scroll through your pipeline and decide who to call. Today you will let a short script do that sort for you, so the list is waiting when you sit down.

You will start from a sample export of leads with a name, a phone number, a stage and the date you last spoke. The script counts how many days each lead has been quiet and puts the longest-quiet leads at the top.

```python
import pandas as pd

leads = pd.read_csv("sample.csv", parse_dates=["last_contact"])
today = pd.Timestamp.today().normalize()
leads["days_quiet"] = (today - leads["last_contact"]).dt.days

follow_up = leads[leads["days_quiet"] >= 7].sort_values("days_quiet", ascending=False)
follow_up[["name", "phone", "days_quiet"]].to_csv("monday_follow_ups.csv", index=False)
print(follow_up)
```
